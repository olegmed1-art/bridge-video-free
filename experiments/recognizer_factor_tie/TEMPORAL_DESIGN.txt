Minimal temporal stability contract - DESIGN ONLY - 2026-10-22

Goal: preserve useful repeated observations without moving card hypotheses
across unproved scene/deal boundaries. Stability never makes a machine card
human-verified, canonical, or part of a complete hand.

Input evidence
Keep source byte verification separate from a supplied source SHA claim.
Each immutable observation has frame SHA, actual PTS and timebase, sequence,
code/model/layout pins, coordinate space, ROI bytes/pixels SHA and raw output.
scene_epoch, deal_epoch and slot association start UNKNOWN. A screen side is
not a canonical seat or a person. A local unknown_index is provenance, not a
track ID: the same pixel crop can move from index 8 to 9 in adjacent samples.

Boundary policy
No propagation until source, PTS, scene/deal scope and assignment evidence
are present. Source/assignment/layout change, a scene/deal boundary, seek,
PTS discontinuity or an unproved gap closes scope BEFORE the next sample.
Pause and occlusion do not add votes; continuity across them needs evidence.
A repeated board number alone does not prove the same deal occurrence.
Do not carry the last readable board ID through an unreadable observation.
max_gap_ms is UNCALIBRATED, not a silently invented constant. Sparse saved
samples with gaps up to 60 seconds cannot qualify temporal continuity.

State and evidence
Use states CLOSED_UNKNOWN, SCOPED_OBSERVATIONS and CONFLICT_UNKNOWN. Retain
raw per-frame history, alternatives, association evidence, vetoes and reasons.
Every track key includes an evidence-bound scope and explicit slot association.
Identical ROI pixel hashes are one visual evidence unit even at different PTS;
keep both historical events. Different pixels alone do not prove independence.
Exact redelivery is idempotent; changed payload under the same event identity
is a conflict. Replay output depends on pinned inputs/policy, not wall clock or
delivery order. A conflict or uncertain slot remains UNKNOWN; do not vote a
disagreement away, move a card across occlusion, fill 13/52 by exclusion, or
infer physical-card identity from the same screen coordinate.

Bounded future evaluation
Start with the seven saved observations in sequence 55-61 and one isolated
earlier control: eight events but seven unique ROI pixel hashes. Freeze human
card/legibility/slot and scene/deal boundary labels independently. UNKNOWN
boundary labels keep propagation closed; unreadable cards are unscorable.
Use separate controls for new scene/deal, pause, seek, UI/layout change,
source/assignment change, redelivery, conflicting payload and occlusion when
existing evidence for them is available. Do not synthesize human labels.

Acceptance
- Zero evidence crosses an unproved scene/deal/source boundary.
- The duplicate-pixel pair remains two events with one visual evidence unit.
- Unknown indices do not define tracks; uncertain associations stay UNKNOWN.
- Frozen inputs and policy reproduce the same history and result.
- Disagreement/occlusion never completes a hidden hand or promotes authority.
- Evaluate boundary errors, association errors, abstention and card errors
  separately on frozen real labels. Current development data cannot measure
  temporal accuracy, general false positives, full hands or production E2E.

Gates: genuine source/PTS and assignment evidence, scene/deal/slot labels,
calibrated association and gap policy, implementation review and a separately
authorized bounded integration. No temporal algorithm is enabled here.
